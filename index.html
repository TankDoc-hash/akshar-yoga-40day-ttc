Everything you need to know about the 40-Day Basic Level Residential Teacher Training Course.

The TTC is a full 40-day intensive course comprising 200 hours of structured learning, daily practice, and teaching methodology.

There are three participation/residential options to fit your needs: Seeker (non-residential for local students), Immersive (comfortable 1 BHK stay, food, and local transit at CSE Residences), and Ultimate (complete sanctuary living at Purantha).

The Seeker (₹1.75 Lakh INR) option includes 40 days of training, all TTC materials, 2 diet/health consultations, 6-month DYC access, 3 Ayurveda therapy sessions at Purantha, special training sessions at Purantha, 6 months Awaken Tribe membership, and 3-day Purantha Camp access. Stay, food, and travel are not included. Review pricing details →

The Immersive (₹3.5 Lakh INR) option includes 40 days training, TTC materials, 1 BHK accommodation at CSE Residences, food, travel, 6 months DYC access, 6 months Awaken Tribe membership, Purantha experiences, and 3-day Purantha Camp access.

The Ultimate (₹5 Lakh INR) option is our premier sanctuary immersion tier. It includes 40 days training, full Purantha campus access, 3 Ayurveda sessions, 2 medicinal showers, 2 tissue healing sessions (subject to expert recommendation), pure Sattvik food, 1 year of Awaken Tribe and DYC access, and 3-day Purantha Camp access.

No prior advanced experience is required. The curriculum begins with foundational alignment and anatomy, making it ideal for both dedicated beginners seeking deep immersion and experienced practitioners preparing to teach professionally.

Upon successful practical and theoretical completion of the 40 days (200 hours), you receive the official Akshar Yoga Basic Level Yoga Teacher Certificate, recognized across the global Akshar Yoga ecosystem.

200-Hour Comprehensive Syllabus

Full TTC Curriculum Breakdown

A systematic progression across practical execution, subtle energetics, and pedagogical mastery.

Module 1: Foundations of Asana & Alignment

60 Hours

Foundational postures, standing, seated, twisting, backbends, and inversions. Emphasis on classical alignment, muscle activation, contraindications, and hands-on adjustments.

Surya Namaskar Variations Standing & Balancing Asanas Spinal Alignment & Joints Hands-on Adjustments

Module 2: Pranayama, Mudras & Subtle Energy

35 Hours

Mastery of traditional breathwork, nadi purification, neuromuscular energy locks (Bandhas), and hand gestures (Hastamudras) for channelizing vital life force.

Nadi Shodhana & Kapalabhati Bhastrika & Ujjayi Hastamudras Mula & Uddiyana Bandhas

Module 3: Yoga Anatomy & Physiology

25 Hours

Practical human anatomy for yoga teachers: musculoskeletal dynamics, respiratory mechanics, nervous system regulation, fascial lines, and injury prevention protocols.

Skeletal & Muscular Systems Respiratory Mechanics Autonomic Nervous System Injury Prevention

Module 4: Yogic Philosophy & Ethics

30 Hours

Deep study of Patanjali's Yoga Sutras, the Eight Limbs of Ashtanga Yoga, Yamas & Niyamas, classical Himalayan Siddha traditions, and contemporary teacher ethics.

Patanjali Yoga Sutras Eight Limbs (Ashtanga) Yamas & Niyamas Teacher Ethics & Sadhana

Module 5: Teaching Methodology & Sequencing

35 Hours

Class structuring, cueing precision, voice modulation, classroom presence, thematic sequencing, adjustments, and guiding students of varying skill levels.

Intelligent Sequencing Vocal Cueing & Presence Class Room Management Student Demonstrations

Module 6: Practicum & Teacher Assessment

15 Hours

Supervised student teaching sessions, hands-on corrections, real-time peer teaching evaluations, mentor feedback, and final practical certification assessment.

Live Practicum Teaching Peer Assessment Instructor Feedback Final Certification Exam
